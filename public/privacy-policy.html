The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run your account and get your shoes to you.
  • Payments go through Paystack. We never see or store your card details.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1.Who we are

Feet-First.ng is an online sneaker store based in Nigeria. We're the data controller for the personal data described here, and we handle it in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

2.What we collect

When you create an account

Your name, email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody at Feet-First.ng can read it.

When you shop

  • Your cart and saved items, so they follow you to any device.
  • For each order: the name, phone number, email and delivery address you give at checkout, any note you add, the items, sizes and prices, and the order's payment and delivery status.

When you pay

Paystack processes your payment. We receive the payment reference, the amount and whether it succeeded. We never receive your card number, PIN or bank login.

When we email you

A record of the emails we send you (for example order confirmations), including whether they were delivered.

Automatically

Like most websites, our server briefly records technical information such as your IP address when you use the site. We use it to keep the site secure, for example to stop repeated failed login attempts.

4.Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with the people and services that help us run the shop, and only what they need:

  • Paystack processes payments.
  • Brevo sends our emails.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores our database, and our hosting provider runs the website.
  • Delivery riders receive your name, phone number and delivery address so they can get your order to you.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our customers and the shop from fraud.

Each of these services may only use your data to do their job for us.

5.Data stored outside Nigeria

Some of the services above (such as our database and email providers) store data on servers outside Nigeria. When that happens, we use providers that protect it to a standard the NDPA accepts, for example through contractual safeguards.

6.How long we keep it

  • Your account, cart and saved items: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Orders and payment records: for as long as Nigerian tax and accounting law requires, usually six years, even if you delete your account.
  • Email records: for as long as they're useful for answering questions about your orders.
  • Security logs: for a short time only.

7.How we protect it

The site uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are hashed, and your login is kept in a secure cookie that other scripts on a page can't read. Repeated login attempts are blocked, and only authorised staff can see order details. No website is completely risk-free, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data, and we'll tell you and the authorities if a breach puts it at risk.

8.Cookies and your browser

We use one essential cookie, which keeps you logged in for up to 7 days. The site can't work without it, and it isn't used for advertising or tracking. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

To save you typing next time, your browser also remembers the delivery details you used at checkout. These stay on your device and you can clear them in your browser settings.

Some of the site's design files load from a public content network (jsDelivr), which sees your IP address when they load, like any website you visit.

9.Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected (you can change your name yourself in your Profile)
  • have your data deleted, apart from records the law requires us to keep
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your data, including stopping marketing emails
  • receive your data in a format you can take elsewhere
  • withdraw any consent you've given

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10.Children

The shop is meant for adults. If you're under 18, please shop with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

11.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our shop or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If the change is significant, we'll let you know by email.
